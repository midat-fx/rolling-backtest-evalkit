# Data sources and licences

| dataset | what it is | size | source | licence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `synthetic` (`data/demand.csv`) | seeded SKU × store weekly demand with promotions | 40 series × 104 weeks | generated by `evalkit data` | MIT (this repo) |
| `hospital` | monthly patient counts for medical products, Jan 2000 – Dec 2006 | 767 series × 84 months | Monash Forecasting Repository, [Zenodo 4656014](https://zenodo.org/records/4656014) | CC BY 4.0 |
| `tourism_monthly` | monthly tourism demand from the Kaggle tourism competition | 366 series × 91–333 months | Monash Forecasting Repository, [Zenodo 4656096](https://zenodo.org/records/4656096) | CC BY 4.0 |

The Monash archives are **not** committed. `evalkit fetch <name>` downloads the
archive, checks its SHA-256 against the value pinned in
`src/evalkit/datasets.py`, and only then parses it (a mismatching download is
deleted). Archives are cached in `$EVALKIT_CACHE` (default `~/.cache/evalkit`);
`--offline` uses the cache only. Pinned checksums:

| archive | SHA-256 | Zenodo MD5 (cross-check) |
|---|---|---|
| `hospital_dataset.zip` | `8fd4085efb4517508e30fc69247eee9b74b300765bc6e94482c5d250efba66e5` | `00ba7422b837555c0715f4245fe608f7` |
| `tourism_monthly_dataset.zip` | `6e434ad8a0ef6acb55ebee0e1d1ffe22707ea4e62c05e091326267f3a5b4a93c` | `7b9a152b2736aff87bb5155f6a650693` |

The test suite runs offline: `tests/fixtures/hospital_sample.tsf` holds the
header and the first 8 series of the hospital file (CC BY 4.0, attribution
below), and the download path is exercised against a local `file://` archive.

Panel schema after parsing: `unique_id, ds, y, promo`, with `ds` an integer
month index from each series' own start and `promo = 0` (no covariates).
